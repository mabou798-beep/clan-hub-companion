Common questions

My player tag is not found. Copy the tag from your in-game profile (it is under your name). Tags use the digit 0 (zero), never the letter O, and never contain I or 1.

Do I need an account? No. The app has no sign-in. Adding your player tag is optional.

How do I remove my saved tag? Open My stats and tap the account button in the top bar, then Remove.

Why are there ads? Ads keep the app free. There are no purchases of any kind.

Some event troops are missing. To avoid showing unreleased content, the app lists only units confirmed live in the game. Event troops appear while players have them.
