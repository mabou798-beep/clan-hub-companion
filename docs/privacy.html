Privacy policy

Clan Hub · last updated 24 October 2026

Clan Hub is a free, unofficial fan app made by Mohamed Abounachit. This policy explains what data the app handles and why.

No accounts

The app has no sign-up or sign-in. We do not ask for your name, email or password.

Your player tag (optional)

If you type a player tag on the My stats screen, it is saved only on your device. When you open that screen, the tag is sent to our small server (a Cloudflare Worker) which forwards it to the game's official public API and returns your public profile. Our server does not log or store tags or profiles. You can remove the tag at any time from My stats.

Advertising

The app shows ads from Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect data such as your device's advertising identifier, approximate location derived from your IP address, and how you interact with ads. On iOS the app asks for permission through App Tracking Transparency before the advertising identifier is used. If you decline, ads are still shown but are not personalised, and no feature is lost. Learn more in Google's advertising policy.

Game data sources

Player, event and league information comes from the game's official public API. Unit stats and art come from the community ClashKing asset library. These requests do not include personal data.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has shared data with us, contact us and we will help remove it.

How to opt out

  • Tracking: Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking, and turn Clan Hub off.
  • Personalised ads: reset or limit your advertising identifier in your device settings.
  • Player tag: remove it in My stats, or delete the app.
