## Description
Your village, the live game calendar and every unit's numbers, in one fast app.

Clan Hub is an unofficial companion for Clash of Clans players. See what is running right now, look up any troop, hero, pet, spell or piece of equipment, and track your own progress with your player tag.

LIVE RIGHT NOW
• Countdowns for Raid Weekend and the current season, refreshed every minute
• Your clan's current war on the home screen once you add your tag

UNITS
• Troops, heroes, pets, spells and equipment for the Home Village and Builder Base
• Hitpoints, damage, unlock level, housing space and upgrade cost at every level
• Star your favourites to keep them on the home screen

MY STATS
• Enter your player tag to see trophies, war stars, donations and Town Hall level
• Upgrade progress for every hero, piece of equipment, troop and spell
• Your tag is saved only on your device. No account or sign-in

DAILY CHALLENGE AND QUIZ
• Five new questions every day, with a streak counter
• Silhouette rounds and questions built from real game numbers

LEAGUES AND WALLPAPERS
• Ranked, Clan War League and Clan Capital ladders with their rules
• High-resolution official fan-kit art to use as wallpapers

Free, with ads. No purchases of any kind.
